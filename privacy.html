Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Who this covers

Claw is a self-hosted assistant used by the members of a single household. It is not offered to the public and has no users outside that household.

What it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent, Claw accesses:

  • Google Calendar — to read and add household events.
  • Gmail (read-only) — to summarise messages already sent to the household's own mailboxes.
  • Basic profile information (name, email address) — used only to identify which account a request belongs to.

Where data goes

Claw runs on hardware owned and operated by the household. Google account data is fetched directly from Google's APIs to that machine and is not sent to any third-party service, other than the AI model provider used to generate responses to the household's own requests.

Data is not sold, rented, shared, or used for advertising. It is not used to train models. No analytics or tracking is performed.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and any cached content are stored on the household's own machine, readable only by the account that runs the assistant. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, which immediately and permanently ends Claw's access.

Limited use

Claw's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
