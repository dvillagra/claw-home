Claw

A private assistant for one household.

Claw is a self-hosted personal assistant run by a single family on their own hardware. It helps with ordinary household logistics: keeping a shared calendar up to date, summarising school and activity announcements, and answering questions about documents the family has saved.

It is not a product, is not offered to the public, and has no users beyond the members of one household.

Google account access

Claw reads the family's own Google Calendar and Gmail using OAuth, with access granted by the account owners and revocable by them at any time from their Google account settings.
